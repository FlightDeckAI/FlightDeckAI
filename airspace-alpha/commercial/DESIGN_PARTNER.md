# Airspace Alpha Founding Design Partner Program

## ICP
Primary:
- event-driven / merger-arbitrage funds
- multi-manager pods
- family offices with event-driven mandates
- alternative-data research teams

Secondary:
- corporate strategy / competitive intelligence
- aerospace / defense research desks
- prediction-market professionals

## Offer
Eight-week paid design partnership.

Deliverables:
- private watch universe
- aircraft / entity ontology
- daily Airspace Tape
- Convergence Radar alerts
- event replay
- weekly analyst brief
- API / CSV export
- measurement of lead time and false-positive rate

## Pricing hypothesis
- Founding Pro: $199–$499/mo for individual researchers
- Alpha Desk Design Partner: $2,500/mo
- Institutional API / custom universe: $5,000–$15,000/mo after proof
- enterprise licensing later

Do not compete on raw ADS-B access.

## Design-partner success metric
A customer should be able to answer:
1. Did Airspace Alpha surface something our existing tools did not?
2. How early?
3. Was the entity attribution correct?
4. Was there a plausible actionable investigation?
5. What percentage of alerts were noise?

## Sales artifact
The strongest demo is Replay:
- physical aircraft event occurs
- Airspace Tape prints it
- ontology explains relationships
- prediction-market prior is shown
- market moves later
- exact lead time is measured

This is the "Bloomberg moment" for the product.
