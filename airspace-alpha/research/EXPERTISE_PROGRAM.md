# LloydPilot / Airspace Alpha Research Program

Objective: establish LloydPilot as a credible practitioner at the intersection of aviation, alternative data, market microstructure and AI.

## Flagship output
**The Airspace Alpha Report**
Quarterly, evidence-first research on what corporate aviation reveals about economic activity.

Recurring sections:
- Airspace Alpha Index: aggregate corporate-flight intensity
- Convergence Watch: unusual multi-entity co-location
- Route Novelty: largest statistically unusual destination changes
- Aviation / Prediction Market Dislocations
- False Positives of the Quarter
- Methodology / calibration update

## Public benchmark
Publish a reproducible subset of the M&A benchmark using only redistributable data and clear licensing boundaries.

## Case-study standard
Every public case contains:
1. what was observable at the time
2. timestamped flight evidence
3. entity-attribution confidence
4. market price at that moment
5. what became public later
6. alternative explanations
7. whether the signal was actually useful

## LloydPilot positioning
Use aviation expertise explicitly:
- explain reliever airports, fuel stops, operating patterns, airspace and weather like a pilot
- explain event studies, priors and calibration like a quantitative researcher
- explain ontology / provenance like an AI engineer

The voice should be: **pilot who understands markets and data**, not finance influencer.
