# Mission Cockpit: making a decision inspectable

**Public demonstration · Synthetic data · Federal.AI**

The workflow asks which aircraft-readiness issue threatens a schedule and what evidence supports a proposed response. The public interface brings together a synthetic scenario, supporting records, risk presentation, and human approval.

## What to evaluate

Can a reader find evidence behind a recommendation? Are missing inputs visible? Does the interface separate an answer from permission to act? Can a reviewer understand the next step without reconstructing the entire conversation?

## Evidence available today

[Open the public cockpit](https://federal.ai/cockpit). The screenshot is the actual public interface; the scenario is synthetic. This brief demonstrates interaction and decision structure. It does not claim a measured operational outcome or customer endorsement.

## A useful engagement

Bring one recurring decision and its source systems. The first task is to define the evidence contract, operating boundary, acceptance cases, and accountable owner.
